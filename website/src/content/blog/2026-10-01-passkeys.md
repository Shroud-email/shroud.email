We've added support for passkeys to Shroud.email. Now, you can sign in without needing a password. Welcome to the future!

Passkeys are a more secure alternative to passwords. Unlike passwords, they are resistant to phishing attacks. Your device or password manager takes care of your passkeys for you, so logging in is as simple as a single click. You can read more about passkeys [here](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/passkeys).

So what are you waiting for? Head to your account's [security settings](https://app.shroud.email/settings/security) and add a passkey today!
